Shipping & Returns

Shipping Information

Domestic Shipping (India)
  • Standard Delivery: 5-7 business days – ₹49
  • Express Delivery: 2-3 business days – ₹99
  • Free Shipping: On orders above ₹500
Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. You will receive a confirmation email with tracking details once your order has shipped.

Return Policy

30-Day Return Guarantee

We want you to love your Swan Botanicals products. If you're not completely satisfied, you may return unused and unopened products within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

How to Return
  1. Email us at returns@swanbotanicals.com with your order number
  2. Receive a return authorization and shipping label
  3. Pack the items securely and ship them back
  4. Refund processed within 5-7 business days of receiving the return
Non-Returnable Items
  • Opened or used products (for hygiene reasons)
  • Products purchased on sale or clearance
  • Gift cards

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective product, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. We will arrange for a replacement or full refund at no additional cost to you.
